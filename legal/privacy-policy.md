# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 31, 2026**

SyncUp is a small, independent app. This policy is written plainly because you deserve to know exactly what happens with your information — no surprises, no fine print.

---

## Who we are

SyncUp is an independent project by Hashir Zahoor. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, write to us at **support@trysyncup.org**.

---

## What information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to function:

- **Email address** — used to create and authenticate your account.
- **Display name** — the name you choose to show your partner.
- **Avatar** — either an emoji you pick or a photo you upload from your library.
- **Goals** — short text entries (up to 140 characters) that you create and share with your partner.
- **Reactions** — emoji reactions you send to your partner's goals.
- **Connection** — the relationship between you and the one person you connect with in the app.
- **Push notification token** — a device identifier issued by Apple that lets us send you notifications. We do not use this for tracking.
- **Crash and error data** — anonymized technical reports if the app crashes. These contain no personal information.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or any other data not listed above.

---

## How we use your information

Everything we collect is used solely to run SyncUp for you:

- Your email authenticates you.
- Your name and avatar are shown to your connected partner.
- Your goals and reactions are displayed to you and your partner only.
- Push tokens deliver notifications about your partner's activity.
- Crash data helps us find and fix bugs.

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral tracking, or marketing of any kind. We do not sell it, share it with data brokers, or use it to build profiles about you.

---

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on **Supabase**, a cloud database service hosted on AWS in the United States. Profile photos you upload are stored in Supabase Storage. Supabase's security practices are documented at supabase.com/security.

---

## Who can see your data

- **You** can see your own profile, goals, and reactions.
- **Your one connected partner** can see your goals, reactions, and avatar. They cannot see your email address.
- **Nobody else** — access to each row of data is enforced at the database level using Row Level Security. Even we cannot read your goal content outside of routine infrastructure maintenance.

---

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an active account. When you delete your account — which you can do at any time from within the app — all your data is permanently removed within 24 hours. We do not retain backups of deleted accounts beyond standard infrastructure recovery windows (up to 7 days).

---

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — write to support@trysyncup.org and we will send you a copy.
- **Delete** your account — use the Delete Account option in the app's Settings screen.
- **Correct** your data — edit your profile directly in the app.
- **Data portability** — request a structured export of your data by emailing support@trysyncup.org.

---

## Children's privacy

SyncUp is not intended for anyone under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at support@trysyncup.org and we will delete it promptly.

---

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data:

- All data is transmitted over TLS (HTTPS).
- Data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure provider.
- Database-level Row Level Security prevents unauthorized access between users.
- API access requires a valid JWT on every request.
- We apply rate limiting on endpoints to prevent abuse.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to support@trysyncup.org.

---

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you with an in-app message before the changes take effect. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last updated.
